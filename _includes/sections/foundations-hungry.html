Hungry,
or just
tired?

Physical hunger and emotional appetite feel almost identical in the moment — but they start from completely different places.

Physical hunger

  • Builds gradually over hours
  • Open to different foods
  • Stops once you're full
  • No guilt afterward

Emotional appetite

  • Hits suddenly, often mid-task
  • Craves one specific dish
  • Keeps going past full
  • Often followed by guilt

"The craving isn't really for the food. It's for what the food used to fix."

Stress, boredom, and even joy can all trigger emotional appetite — which is part of why comfort eating isn't just a "bad habit" to fix, but a coping mechanism that's usually working, at least in the short term.
