Editor's note

This project started with a pretty basic observation: every one of us has ordered the same 2am maggi during exam week, without ever really asking why. Turns out there's real psychology behind it — reward pathways, memory, even a bit of evolution. This issue is us trying to actually understand that, one section at a time.

— The editorial team
